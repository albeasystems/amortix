FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What types of mortgage does Amortix calculate? ▼
Amortix calculates fixed-rate, variable-rate and mixed mortgages using the French amortization method (equal monthly payments), which is the standard used by the majority of lenders.
Does it calculate APR? ▼
Yes. Amortix calculates both the nominal interest rate and the APR (Annual Percentage Rate), taking into account any fees and associated costs you enter.
Can I export the amortization schedule? ▼
Yes, you can export the full amortization schedule as a PDF and share it directly from the app with any application on your iPhone — email, WhatsApp, Files, and more.
Does the app store my data in the cloud? ▼
No. All your mortgage data is stored only on your device. Amortix does not require an account or an internet connection to work.
Is it available on Android? ▼
Amortix is currently available only for iPhone (iOS 16+). We are considering Android support in a future release.
Is it really completely free? ▼
Yes, 100% free. No ads, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases. All features are available from the moment you download the app, at no cost whatsoever.
