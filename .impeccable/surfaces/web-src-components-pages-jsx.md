# Surface brief: Amortix website (home + four calculator pages, nine languages)

Mode: Persuade (home) with working tools. Visitor arrives from a search or an AI answer asking what a mortgage really costs, whether to prepay, or what a rate rise does.

Audience, job, action: person with or about to sign a mortgage; wants the number first, then the app. Action: use the calculator, then download from the App Store. Proof: only real captures (iOS Simulator, nine languages) and figures computed by a port of the app's engine; no ratings, testimonials or press exist.

Constraints: inherits the established sibling world of Capital's website (paper and ink, Geist, statement rows, hairlines, flat surfaces, phone-only shadow). Amortix keeps one blue (capital, actions) and swaps Capital's red for amber (interest). Free with ads; one purchase removes ads; no price shown. Static HTML prerendered, SEO and llms.txt first-class.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The first thing a visitor sees is where each euro goes: one bar split into capital and interest, drawn to scale from the app's own example. Refuses the fintech default of gradient hero plus three icon cards.
OWN-WORLD: Near-white paper, near-black ink, Geist Variable, hairline zones, filled form fields with a ruled underline, statement rows with dotted leaders, flat panels. Blue is the capital you borrow and the actions; amber is interest and the one hot row. Dark mode is the same roles on near-black.
STORY: Visitor understands the split between capital and interest, tries their own numbers, sees the real app walk-through in their language, and downloads. Believes it because every figure is computable and every screen is a real capture.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Headline left at display scale, lede, App Store stamp, the capital/interest bar with its two figures; right, the app's real journey loop in a phone frame (about 17rem wide). Primary action sits under the lede.
FORM: Inherited Capital grammar, seed key none (inherited world, no roll), first of one.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
